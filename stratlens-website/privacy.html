Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025  |  Domain: stratlens.site

StratLens ("we", "our", "us") operates the mobile application StratLens and the website at stratlens.site. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can exercise your rights.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account data: email address, display name, password hash (managed by Supabase Auth).
  • Usage data: pages viewed, events opened, filters applied, session duration.
  • Preferences: watched regions, watched categories, alert settings.
  • Device data: device type, OS version, push notification token.
  • Payment data: billing is handled by a third-party processor; we do not store card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Provision and personalise the StratLens service.
  • Send region-based alerts and notifications you have subscribed to.
  • Improve product features through aggregated, anonymised analytics.
  • Communicate service updates, security notices, and support responses.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Supabase — authentication and database infrastructure.
  • Anthropic — AI summarisation (event text only; no PII sent).
  • Payment processor — billing transactions (to be confirmed before launch).
  • Law enforcement when required by applicable law.

4. Data Retention

Account data is retained for the lifetime of your account plus 90 days after deletion. Anonymised analytics may be retained indefinitely.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have the right to:

  • Access, rectify, or delete your personal data.
  • Object to or restrict processing.
  • Data portability.
  • Lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

To exercise any right, contact us at the address below.

6. Cookies & Tracking

The StratLens website uses minimal, essential cookies for session management. No third-party advertising trackers are used.

7. Children's Privacy

StratLens is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated by email or in-app notice. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
